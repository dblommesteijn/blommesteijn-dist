mbatterier

The missing power-boost and battery manager for Mac

Keeps a MacBook in Low Power by default and gives High Power only when you ask for it or the battery is full enough.

$ mbat boost 1h  # High Power for an hour
$ mbat watch     # by charge: 80% / 20%
$ mbat low       # back to Low Power
